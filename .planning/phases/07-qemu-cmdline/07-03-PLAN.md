<objective>

Complete the nested-bus device dispatch that Plan 07-02 started: walk `Q35Chipset`'s remaining three
bus maps (`pci_bus`, `sata_bus`, `ide_bus`) plus `usb_bus`, wiring `GenericPciDevice`, sata/ide
storage (reusing Plan 07-02's `storage.rs`), `PvScsi`-on-pci_bus (reusing Plan 07-02's
`emit_pvscsi`), and `GenericUsbDevice` into the emitter. Also close the import-side half of `D-01`'s
"ALL root devices... storage/network/usb" intent by wiring `ProxmoxImporter::into_runtime()` to
actually construct `VirtioNetPcie` / `GenericUsbDevice` entries from `ProxmoxVmConf.net` / `.usb` —
confirmed by RESEARCH.md as parsed-but-dropped today.

- **Purpose:** Finish full device-family coverage (`QEMU-01`) across every bus `Q35Chipset` models, and
  make the emitter demonstrably exercisable end-to-end on real network/USB data (not just
  synthetic), which also unblocks Plan 07-05's ordering test from needing purely-synthetic netdev
  coverage.
- **Output:** `src/config/qemu/handlers/pci.rs`, `src/config/qemu/handlers/usb.rs` created; `storage.rs`
  gains sata/ide emission functions; `root.rs`'s `Chipset` arm walks all 5 bus maps;
  `ProxmoxImporter::into_runtime()` parses `net` / `usb` into the chipset.

</objective>
