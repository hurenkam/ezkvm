<objective>

Implement the PCIe-bus branch of the nested-device dispatch that Plan 07-01 deliberately left as a
no-op (`// bus walk added in Plan 07-02/07-03`): walk `Q35Chipset.pcie_bus`, dispatch on
`PcieBusDeviceKind` for all four variants (`PvScsi`, `HostPci`, `Ivshmem`, `VirtioNet`), and — for
`PvScsi` — recurse one level deeper into its `scsi_bus` to emit scsi drive+device pairs. Also stand
up the shared `storage.rs` drive+device emission module that Plan 07-03 reuses for `pci_bus`'s
`PvScsi`, `sata_bus`, and `ide_bus`.

- **Purpose:** Prove the two-level dispatch pattern (root devices → nested bus maps) on the bus family
  with the most device kinds (4 of 4 `PcieBusDeviceKind` variants), establishing the shared
  storage-emission shape before Plan 07-03 extends it to the remaining three bus types and USB.
- **Output:** `src/config/qemu/handlers/pcie.rs` and `src/config/qemu/handlers/storage.rs` created;
  `src/config/qemu/handlers/root.rs`'s `Chipset` arm extended to walk `pcie_bus`;
  `src/config/qemu/handlers.rs` declares `mod pcie;` and `mod storage;`.

</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-03 | Tampering | `HostPci` `base_bdf`/`functions` formatted directly into `-device vfio-pci,host=...` | low | accept | `HostPci` fields originate from an already-parsed, already-typed Proxmox `.conf` (Phase 3/4 boundary) or ezkvm-YAML; no untrusted raw text reaches this handler — same rationale as T-07-01 in Plan 07-01 |
| T-07-04 | Denial of Service | `Ivshmem` `mem_path`/`size` passed verbatim into `-object memory-backend-file` | low | accept | Local single-user process; a malformed path/size only affects the local VM the operator controls, not a network-facing or multi-tenant surface |

No threat in this plan meets the `high` severity bar under ASVS level 1 — no authentication,
session, network-input, or cryptography surface. No blocking checkpoint required.

</threat_model>
