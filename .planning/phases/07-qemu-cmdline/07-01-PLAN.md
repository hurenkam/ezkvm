<objective>

Replace the `src/config/qemu.rs` stub with the foundation of the segmented QEMU commandline emitter: `QemuCommandLineBuilder`, `QemuContext`, the `TryFrom<(Runtime, QemuContext)>` dispatch skeleton, and handlers for root-level devices needing no nested-bus recursion (`Memory`, `Chipset` machine-type flag, `EfiDisk`, `TpmState`, `AudioDevice`, `SpiceDisplay`, `RawArgs`).

It also closes two `Runtime` data-model gaps required by `D-02`/ROADMAP that no prior phase modeled: boot order (`D-07`) and CPU topology (`-smp`/`-cpu`) plus the Proxmox `vga: none` flag.

**Purpose:** Establish the segment-ordering foundation (`QEMU-02`'s structural guarantee) and prove the `TryFrom` path end-to-end on a real, runnable subset before the heavier nested-bus dispatch (Plans 07-02/07-03) builds on top of it.

**Output:**

- `src/config/qemu.rs` rewritten
- `src/config/qemu/{builder,handlers}.rs` + `handlers/root.rs` created
- `Runtime`/`RuntimeBuilder` gain `boot_order`, `CpuTopology`, `VgaConfig`
- `ProxmoxImporter` parses `boot`, `cores`, `sockets`, `cpu`, `vga`

</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-01 | Tampering | `RawArgs` verbatim passthrough (`handlers/root.rs`) | low | accept | `RawArgs` originates from an already-parsed, already-typed Proxmox `.conf` (Phase 3/4 boundary); Phase 7 never receives untrusted raw text, so no injection surface exists at this layer — rationale documented in `D-06`/RESEARCH.md Security Domain |
| T-07-02 | Information Disclosure | `QemuContext` paths surfaced via `.expect()` panic messages | low | accept | Panic messages may include local filesystem paths (socket/storage paths); this is single-user local process diagnostic output, not a network-facing or multi-tenant surface — acceptable per `D-06`'s "programmer error" framing |

No threat in this plan meets the `high` severity bar under ASVS level 1 — this phase has no authentication, session, network-input, or cryptography surface (confirmed in 07-RESEARCH.md's Security Domain section). No blocking checkpoint required.
